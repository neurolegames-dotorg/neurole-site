The Synapse

How to Play

Find groups of four items that share something in common.

Select four tiles, then press Submit.

Categories range from straightforward to tricky, some items could fit more than one group.

Mistakes

You get four mistakes before the puzzle ends. If you're one away from a correct group, we'll let you know.

Tips

Start with the group you're most confident about.

Watch out for words that seem to belong to an obvious category, they're often a trap.
